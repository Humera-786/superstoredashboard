About this project

Portfolio documentation.

Business problem

Retail teams need to understand which products, categories and regions generate sales and profit, and whether discounts are associated with lower profitability.

Input

CSV transaction records containing Sales, Profit, Quantity, Order Date and dimensions such as Region, Category, Product and Customer.

Output

Interactive dashboard, charts, ranked tables, automated findings, data-quality checks and exportable filtered rows.

Workflow
  1. Load transaction data.
  2. Inspect blanks, duplicates and dates.
  3. Calculate sales, profit and quantity.
  4. Compare trends, regions, products, customers and discounts.
  5. Export filtered data or print a PDF.
Tools demonstrated

Excel · SQL · Python/Pandas · HTML/CSS/JavaScript · Power BI

Important: Results are calculated from the CSV you select. This website does not automatically connect to SQL, Python or Power BI. Confirm findings against your analysis before publishing. Some features need specific columns in your file.
